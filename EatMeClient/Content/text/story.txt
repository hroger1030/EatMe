
The old pac king is dying. 

He has long ruled the Land, but he has no heir. 

His kingdom is two realms. The Red Pacs live to the east and the Blue Pacs live to the west. Each believes the crown belongs to them.

So the king made one final decree: a single game of capture the flag. Whichever side carries the enemy’s banner the most will inherit the throne.

Today is the great flag battle, go claim the crown!
